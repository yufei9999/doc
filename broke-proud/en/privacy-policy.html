Broke Proud

Privacy Policy · Last updated October 16, 2026

The short version

Broke Proud is a paid, single-player game that works entirely offline. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. Nothing you do in the app leaves your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits.

Data we collect

None. Broke Proud never asks for your name, email, phone number, age, location, contacts, payment information, or any other personal information. The app contains no sign-up or login screens and makes no network requests.

Data stored on your device

All game data is stored locally on your device only, in the app's own private storage (system user defaults and local app files). This includes:

  • Game settings (sound and haptics toggles)
  • Achievements, titles, and lifetime statistics
  • Your most recent run summary
  • A local backup copy of the above, used only to recover from a corrupted save

This data never leaves the device, is not included in any system crash or diagnostics report controlled by us, and cannot be accessed by us or any third party. Resetting all data from the in-app Settings screen permanently erases it from the device.

Photos

Broke Proud can save a spending-receipt poster image to your photo library, but only when you tap "Save Receipt". This uses an add-only permission: the app writes one image you choose to save and cannot read, browse, or upload your photos. We never access, collect, or transmit any images from your photo library.

Purchases

Broke Proud is a one-time paid download. There are no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, no consumables, and no storefront inside the app. We do not process or receive any payment details; purchases are handled entirely by Apple under the App Store terms.

Advertising and tracking

There are no ads and no advertising or tracking technologies. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not use App Tracking Transparency, and does not contact any advertising or analytics services.

Third-party services

None. Broke Proud integrates no third-party SDKs, analytics platforms, crash reporters, authentication providers, or cloud services.

Internet connectivity

The app is fully functional in Airplane Mode and does not require an internet connection at any time.

Children

Broke Proud is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. Because the app collects no data of any kind, it also collects no data from children. A parent does not need to set up or manage anything.

Security

Because no data is transmitted or stored outside your device, there is no server-side database to breach. Your game data is protected by the built-in security of iOS and your device passcode.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. The app only receives updates through the official App Store release process; there is no remote configuration or hot-updating.

Support

Broke Proud is designed to need no account or configuration. If you have a question, found a bug, or need a refund, please email us and include your device model and iOS version.

Tips:

  • The game can be played completely offline — check Airplane Mode if you are unsure about privacy.
  • Use "Reset All Data" in Settings to erase all local progress and start fresh.
  • If saving a receipt poster fails, grant photo-library add permission in iOS Settings → Broke Proud.
  • For billing and refund requests, Apple handles all payments: contact Apple Support at support.apple.com/billing.
