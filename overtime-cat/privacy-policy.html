Privacy Policy

Overtime Cat · Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Summary

Overtime Cat works offline and needs no account. We collect no personal data. Anonymous gameplay statistics are collected only if you opt in. There are no ads and no third-party tracking SDKs in this version.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data stored only on your device

  • Best score and games played
  • Settings (sound, haptics, multi-touch, language)
  • Consent choice and any leaderboard submissions awaiting retry

These never leave your device unless you explicitly share a result poster.

2.2 Optional anonymous analytics (consent-gated)

If you tap “Allow anonymous stats”, we collect aggregate gameplay events:

EventWhat it contains
App openStorefront region, language, OS version, first-launch flag
Game start / endRound duration, score, best combo, critical hits
TapSampled at roughly 1 in 10 taps
Meme shown / share tappedContent pack version, share channel
Consent choiceWhether you granted or denied analytics
  • Events are linked to a random UUID generated on your device and stored in the iOS Keychain. It is not your Apple identifier and cannot be used to identify you.
  • We do not use IDFA, device fingerprints, or location.
  • Analytics data is retained for no more than 90 days.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (see §8); future app versions will also expose this in Settings.

2.3 Leaderboards

  • Game Center: scores are submitted to and governed by Apple’s Game Center under Apple’s Privacy Policy. We never receive your Apple identity.
  • Global board (optional): we store only a random anonymous ID, your score, a timestamp, and a coarse region. No nickname, email, or profile is stored.

2.4 Photo library

“Save poster” uses the iOS add-only photo permission (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription) to write the result image you request. We cannot read your photo library.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • No account registration or login
  • No advertising or ad SDKs in version 1.0.0 (future ad-enabled versions will ask consent first via Apple’s ATT and Google’s UMP flows, and this policy will be updated)
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No sale or sharing of personal information
  • No collection from users known to be under the age of digital consent

4. Legal Bases (EEA/UK)

Where the GDPR applies: analytics are processed based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time; strictly necessary local storage (your settings) relies on legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

5. Your Rights

Depending on your region (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, APPI, PDPL and others), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export data, object to processing, or opt out of “sharing” as defined by local law. Because we hold no identifying data, most requests are satisfied by simply deleting the app. To exercise any right, contact us at the email below and we will respond within the statutory period.

6. International Transfers

Anonymous analytics and leaderboard data may be processed outside your country. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK, Japan, Korea, Saudi Arabia, UAE), transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses, and a summary is provided in the relevant localized version of this policy.

7. Children

Overtime Cat is a casual game not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect app changes (for example, if ads or the global leaderboard are enabled). Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
