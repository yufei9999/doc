关于 Flit

Flit 是一款轻量的本地想法卡片应用：随手记下想到的事，随时翻看、整理，所有内容只留在你的设备上。

应用完全离线运行，不发起任何网络请求，也不使用服务器；不需要账号，也没有登录与订阅。

常见问题

数据保存在哪里？删除后能否恢复？

所有卡片只保存在设备本地沙盒的 cards.json 及其备份文件中，不上传、不同步到任何地方。在应用内删除的卡片会立即从本地文件中移除；卸载应用时，这些文件也会一并删除。删除后无法在应用内恢复，请谨慎操作。如果你此前做过 iCloud 或电脑备份，能否恢复取决于你的备份设置与备份时间，需要通过系统备份来恢复设备。

为什么需要麦克风与语音识别权限？

只用于语音输入：点按语音按钮后，把你说的话转成卡片文字。应用只在你第一次使用语音输入时请求权限，不会在启动时或后台开启麦克风，也不会把录音保存成文件。拒绝授权不影响其他功能，你仍然可以用键盘输入。

语音识别会上传吗？会保存音频吗？

不会。语音输入强制使用系统在设备端完成识别，音频不离开你的设备、不上传到 Apple 服务器或传给我们，应用也不会生成或保留任何音频文件。只有识别出的文字会写入本地的 cards.json。

系统听写未开启或设备端识别不可用时怎么办？

此时语音输入会停用，并提示「设备端语音识别当前不可用，请检查系统听写设置或改用键盘」。你可以前往 iOS「设置」>「通用」>「键盘」，确认「启用听写」已打开，并已下载当前语言的听写资源；也可以直接改用键盘输入。应用不会改用需要联网的识别方式。

是否需要联网或账号？

都不需要。应用完全离线运行，不发起任何网络请求、不使用服务器，也没有账号、登录或订阅。

支持哪些语言？如何切换？

界面支持简体中文、繁体中文、英语、日语、韩语、德语、西班牙语、葡萄牙语和阿拉伯语，跟随 iOS 系统语言（「设置」>「通用」>「语言与地区」）；系统语言不在支持范围内时，界面以简体中文显示。应用内也有语言选项，但它只影响日期格式，不改变界面文案。

支持的最低 iOS 版本是多少？

iOS 17.0 或更高版本。

About Flit

Flit is a lightweight, local idea-card app: jot down what comes to mind, browse and tidy it up later — everything stays on your device.

The app works fully offline, makes no network requests and uses no server. There is no account, sign-in or subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored, and can deleted data be recovered?

All cards are stored only in cards.json and its backup file inside the app's local sandbox. Nothing is uploaded or synced anywhere. Cards you delete in the app are removed from the local files immediately, and uninstalling the app deletes those files as well. Deleted data cannot be recovered inside the app, so please be careful. If you previously made an iCloud or computer backup, whether it can be restored depends on your backup settings and when the backup was made, and restoring requires the system backup.

Why does the app need microphone and speech recognition permission?

Only for voice input: after you tap the voice button, what you say is turned into card text. The permission is requested only the first time you use voice input; the app never opens the microphone at launch or in the background, and it does not save a recording as a file. Declining does not affect any other feature — you can still type.

Is my speech uploaded? Is audio stored?

No. Voice input uses on-device speech recognition only. Audio never leaves your device and is never uploaded to Apple's servers or to us, and the app creates or keeps no audio files. Only the recognised text is written to the local cards.json.

What if system dictation is off or on-device recognition is unavailable?

Voice input is then turned off and the app shows: "On-device speech recognition is currently unavailable. Please check your system dictation settings or use the keyboard." You can go to iOS Settings > General > Keyboard, make sure Dictation is on and the dictation resources for your language have been downloaded, or simply type instead. The app never switches to a recognition method that needs a network connection.

Does the app need a network connection or an account?

Neither. The app runs fully offline, makes no network requests and uses no server. There is no account, sign-in or subscription.

Which languages are supported, and how do I switch?

The interface is available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, Spanish, Portuguese and Arabic, and follows the iOS system language (Settings > General > Language & Region). If your system language is not supported, the interface appears in Simplified Chinese. The app also offers a language option, but it only affects date formatting, not the interface text.

What is the minimum iOS version?

iOS 17.0 or later.
